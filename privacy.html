Privacy Policy for Ghost Match

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Ghost Match ("the App") is a free, family-friendly Halloween ghost-matching puzzle game developed by Parker Data Link LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

Summary

Ghost Match does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data. The App runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs integrated into the App.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect: names, email addresses, device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. The App does not require an internet connection to play after it has loaded, and no gameplay data is sent to any server.

Local Storage

The App may use your device's local storage (for example, to remember your best score or game settings) so your progress persists between sessions. This information stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Children's Privacy

Because Ghost Match collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third-Party Services

Ghost Match does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or data-collection services. If this ever changes in a future update, we will update this policy and the App's store listing accordingly before the change takes effect.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at parkerp2020@gmail.com.
